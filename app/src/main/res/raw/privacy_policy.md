# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

{{APP_NAME}} is a personal reading tracker. It is built to work without an account, and your reading data stays on your device. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, and why.

## The short version

- No account, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs. Signing in with Google is only needed for the optional Drive sync.
- Your library, reading sessions, notes and settings are stored on your device, and in your own Google Drive only if you turn on Drive sync.
- The developer does not receive, collect or sell any of your data.

## Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your phone:

- Books you add (title, authors, cover, page counts, status, ratings, reviews, collections, loans and purchases).
- Reading sessions (start and end times, pages read, where you read).
- Notes, quotes and highlights you write, scan or dictate.
- Your preferences (goals, theme, reminder time, app lock setting).
- Cover photos you take with the camera.

You can delete any book, note or session in the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

## Data that leaves your device

- **Book search and barcode lookup.** When you search for a book or scan a barcode, the search text or ISBN is sent to the Google Books API and, if needed, the Open Library API to fetch book details. Book covers are then downloaded from those services. These requests do not include your library or any personal information, but the services receive your IP address as part of any internet request. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Internet Archive (Open Library) privacy policy.
- **Handwriting recognition.** The first time you use handwritten notes, the app downloads a recognition model from Google. Recognition then runs on your device.
- **Translation.** The first time you translate between two languages, the app downloads a translation model for that pair from Google. Language detection and translation then run on your device; the text you translate is not sent anywhere.
- **Page scanner.** "Clean page" and cover scanning use Google's document scanner, provided by Google Play services on your phone. The scan is processed on your device and handed back to the app; it is not uploaded.
- **Drive sync (optional).** If you connect Google Drive in Settings, a copy of your library, sessions, notes, vocabulary, review progress, cover photos and note photos is stored in a private app folder in your own Google Drive, so your other devices can sync with it. Only this app can read that folder, and the developer never receives it. Turning sync off stops further uploads; you can delete the stored copy from your Google account (Drive settings, Manage apps).
- **Watch sync.** If you use the Wear OS companion app, your current books, progress and dictated notes are exchanged between your phone and watch through Google Play services. This data travels only between your own devices.
- **Backups.** Android may include the app's data in your device's automatic backup to your Google account, if you have backup turned on. Backup files you export from Settings are saved wherever you choose and are under your control.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — to scan book barcodes, capture text from pages (on-device text recognition) and take cover photos. Images used for scanning are processed on your device and are not stored or sent anywhere.
- **Notifications** — to show the reading timer and the optional daily reminder.
- **Do Not Disturb access** (optional) — only if you turn on "Deep focus", to silence notifications while you read.
- **Biometrics / screen lock** (optional) — only if you turn on App lock. Authentication is handled entirely by Android; the app never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN.
- **Internet** — for book search, barcode lookup, cover images, downloading recognition and translation models, and the optional Drive sync.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
